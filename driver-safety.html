Key Driver Safety Responsibilities

  • Complete daily vehicle checks before starting a journey.
  • Report vehicle defects immediately.
  • Follow legal driving hours and rest break requirements.
  • Wear appropriate PPE when required.
  • Drive according to road, weather and traffic conditions.

Fatigue Management

Driver tiredness can increase the risk of accidents. Drivers should take regular breaks, follow working time rules and report concerns if they feel unfit to drive.

Safe Working Culture

A positive safety culture depends on drivers, clerks and managers working together. Clear communication and accurate reporting help protect staff, vehicles and the public.
