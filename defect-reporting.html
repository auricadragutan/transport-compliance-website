Why Defect Reporting Matters

Vehicle defect reporting is essential because it helps prevent accidents, protects drivers and ensures vehicles meet legal road safety standards.

Common Defects to Check

  • Tyres, wheels and wheel nuts
  • Lights and indicators
  • Brakes and steering
  • Mirrors and windows
  • Leaks, warning lights and body damage

Reporting Process

  1. Identify the defect during the vehicle check.
  2. Record the defect accurately.
  3. Inform the transport office or manager.
  4. Do not use the vehicle if it is unsafe.
  5. Ensure the defect is repaired and signed off before use.
